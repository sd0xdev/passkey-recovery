# Code of conduct

This project is a small, volunteer effort to help people recover their own funds. Many people who arrive here are stressed and may have lost money. Treat everyone with patience and respect.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good faith. Explain, do not belittle.
- Keep discussion about the work. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Protect other people's safety: never ask anyone for a seed phrase, private key, password or passkey, and point out anyone who does.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or sexualised content.
- Publishing someone's private information, wallet addresses or transactions without their consent.
- Scams: impersonating the maintainer or Coinbase, offering "recovery services", or linking to look-alike sites.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content and block accounts that break these rules. Report a problem privately through the channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or by opening an issue that asks for a private contact without details. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies to the repository, its issues and discussions, and any space that represents the project.
